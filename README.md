# Amazon Products Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Amazon Products Sales Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. It provides an overview of sales performance, product category contribution, weekly and monthly sales trends, and product review metrics.

The dashboard transforms sales data into meaningful visualizations and KPIs to support data-driven business analysis and decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze year-to-date (YTD) and quarter-to-date (QTD) sales performance.
- Track monthly and weekly sales trends.
- Identify high-performing product categories.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Identify the top five products by YTD sales.
- Identify the top five products by YTD reviews.
- Enable interactive analysis using product category and quarter filters.

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| YTD Sales | Total sales revenue for the year to date |
| QTD Sales | Total sales revenue for the quarter to date |
| YTD Products Sold | Total products sold year to date |
| YTD Reviews | Review metric reported by the dashboard |

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- **Sales by Month:** Visualizes monthly sales trends.
- **Sales by Week:** Compares sales performance across weeks.
- **Sales by Product Category:** Displays YTD sales, QTD sales, and percentage contribution.
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Sales:** Highlights leading products by sales revenue.
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews:** Highlights products with the highest review metric.
- **Interactive Filters:** Enables filtering by product category and quarter.

## ❓ Business Questions

1. What are the YTD and QTD sales?
2. How do sales change throughout the year?
3. Which weeks have the highest and lowest sales?
4. Which product categories contribute the most to sales?
5. What percentage of YTD sales comes from each category?
6. Which five products generate the highest YTD sales?
7. Which five products have the highest review counts?
8. How does sales performance vary by quarter?
9. Which categories could offer opportunities for further analysis?

## 🔍 Key Observations

- Sales show noticeable peaks in September and November–December in the displayed monthly trend.
- Weekly sales fluctuate, with several higher-sales weeks toward the latter part of the displayed period.
- Men Clothes and Camera show comparatively high YTD sales among the visible product categories.
- Product rankings provide a way to compare sales performance with customer review activity.

*These observations are based on the displayed dashboard and should be validated against the underlying dataset before being treated as business conclusions.*
